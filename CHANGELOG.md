# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-11

First release. Changedetection.io 0.60.9 in Container Manager, password
protection from the first start (password shown once), no example watches,
and a DSM window: Watches (last change, last check and errors, with search;
add a page by its address, check now, check all, pause and resume; pages on
your own network on or off; the optional Chrome browser for JavaScript pages
on or off), Alerts (DSM notifications for changes and failed checks, with a
test; the ntfy package on this NAS connected in one click through a
write-only sender; Changedetection.io's Apprise addresses listed without
their secrets, added, tested and removed), Access (a new password, your own,
or none), backups with restore, updates that move the pinned release on,
storage, a redacted support report, and full removal.
